# VistaraBI AI Chat: The Ultimate User Guide

Welcome to the **VistaraBI AI Chat Assistant**! Our state-of-the-art AI engine (Module 6) allows you to interact with your business data through natural, conversational language. You don't need to write SQL, learn technical jargon, or know exactly how your data is structured. 

Just ask questions, and the AI will analyze, correlate, and visualize the answers for you in real-time.

---

## 🌟 Core Capabilities

Our AI chat isn't just a simple Q&A bot. It is powered by a multi-stage intelligence pipeline designed to find root causes, detect anomalies, and build dashboards on the fly.

### 1. 📊 Natural Language Charting & Dashboarding
You can command the AI to build or modify your dashboard directly from the chat. It understands what type of chart works best for your data.

*   **Create Charts:** *"Create a bar chart showing Sales vs. Marketing Spend."*
*   **Modify Dashboard:** *"Add a KPI card for Customer Churn to my dashboard."*
*   **Cross-Filtering:** *"Filter my dashboard to only show data from Q3 2023."*
*   **Change Granularity:** *"Show me the revenue chart grouped by week instead of month."*

### 2. 🔍 Anomaly & Event Detection
The AI actively monitors your data for unusual patterns. You can ask it to investigate spikes, drops, or general anomalies.

*   *"Are there any anomalies in our expenses this year?"*
*   *"Why did sales drop so suddenly in November?"*
*   *"Highlight the biggest positive trends in user growth this quarter."*

### 3. 🔗 Correlation & Root Cause Analysis (Advanced)
The AI doesn't just look at one metric at a time; it looks for hidden relationships across your entire business using advanced correlation and time-lag engines.

*   **Direct Correlation:** *"Is there a correlation between customer support tickets and user churn?"*
*   **Time-Lag Analysis:** *"Does an increase in ad spend affect our sales in the following week?"*
*   **Root Cause:** *"Did the summer promotion actually cause the spike in inventory turnover, or was it a seasonal trend?"*

### 4. 🧠 Transparent Math & Data Lineage
Our AI is "hallucination-free" because it operates on strict numeric guards. If you ever doubt a number, you can ask the AI to show its work.

*   *"How exactly are you calculating the Customer Lifetime Value here?"*
*   *"Show me the formula and the data tables you used for this insight."*
*   *"Explain this metric in simple terms."*

## 🏢 Querying Across the 8 Business Domains

VistaraBI automatically categorizes your uploaded data into one of 8 specific business domains. The AI uses this context to understand standard industry metrics automatically.

Here is how you can leverage the AI based on your specific domain:

### 1. 🛒 E-Commerce
*Focuses on orders, products, carts, and customer purchasing behavior.*
*   *"What is our Average Order Value (AOV) over time?"*
*   *"Is there a correlation between shipping delays and negative reviews?"*
*   *"Create a line chart of cart abandonment rates for the last 30 days."*

### 2. 💻 SaaS (Software as a Service)
*Focuses on subscriptions, recurring revenue, and user retention.*
*   *"What is our current Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)?"*
*   *"Show me a scatter plot of Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs. Lifetime Value (LTV)."*
*   *"Why did churn increase after the latest pricing update?"*

### 3. 🎓 EdTech (Educational Technology)
*Focuses on students, course enrollments, progress, and performance.*
*   *"What is the average completion rate for the 'Intro to AI' course?"*
*   *"Does time spent on the platform correlate with higher assessment scores?"*
*   *"Filter the dashboard to only show students enrolled in 2024."*

### 4. 🏪 Retail
*Focuses on physical inventory, point-of-sale transactions, and store performance.*
*   *"Which store location has the highest inventory turnover?"*
*   *"Are any of our products hitting their reorder point?"*
*   *"Chart the relationship between in-store promotions and foot traffic."*

### 5. 🧾 Services (Agencies & Consultancies)
*Focuses on billable hours, project budgets, and client management.*
*   *"What is our current resource utilization rate?"*
*   *"Which client project has the highest profit margin?"*
*   *"Show me a breakdown of billable vs. non-billable hours this month."*

### 6. 🏭 Manufacturing
*Focuses on production batches, quality control, machine maintenance, and output.*
*   *"What is the defect rate on Production Line 3?"*
*   *"Calculate our Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)."*
*   *"Does machine maintenance frequency correlate with fewer production bottlenecks?"*

### 7. 🏥 Healthcare
*Focuses on patients, appointments, treatments, and facility management.*
*   *"What is the average patient wait time in the ER?"*
*   *"Show me the breakdown of billing amounts by insurance provider in a donut chart."*
*   *"Is there a trend in seasonal appointment cancellations?"*

### 8. 💰 Finance
*Focuses on accounts, transactions, loans, portfolios, and risk.*
*   *"What is the total value of our asset portfolio?"*
*   *"Graph the risk scores of approved vs. rejected loan applications."*
*   *"Are there any anomalous transaction volumes from yesterday?"*

## 💡 Pro Tips for Power Users

1.  **Combine Commands:** You can ask multi-part questions. *"Find the anomaly in Q2 sales, explain why it happened, and create a chart showing the trend."*
2.  **Use Timeframes:** The AI excels at comparing periods. Use phrases like *"Year over Year" (YoY)*, *"Month over Month" (MoM)*, or *"Year to Date" (YTD)*.
3.  **Synthesize Insights:** If you have a complex dashboard, ask the AI to summarize it. *"Synthesize the key takeaways from my current dashboard view."* (This triggers our advanced Synthesis Engine).
4.  **Trust the Math (Numeric Guards):** VistaraBI uses cross-packet numeric guards to ensure the AI doesn't hallucinate numbers. If it gives you a figure, it matches your raw data precisely.
