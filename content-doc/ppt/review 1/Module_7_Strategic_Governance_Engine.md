# Module 7: AI-Driven Strategic Governance & Goal Engine
## Theoretical Foundation & Architecture

Module 7 represents the "Strategic Intelligence" of VistaraBI. It shifts the platform from descriptive (what happened) to prescriptive (what should we do?) analytics.

### 1. The Goal Strategy Engine (GSE)
*   **Theoretical Goal:** Translating high-level business objectives into tactical plans.
*   **Goal Parser:** Extracts metrics, targets, and timeframes from a natural language goal (e.g., *"Reduce operational costs by 15% within 6 months"*).
*   **KPI Mapping:** Ensures that the goal is tied to a validated metric within the KPI Blueprint, preventing "Shadow Metrics."

### 2. Goal Decomposition & Pathfinding
*   **Theoretical Goal:** Mathematical factorization of business success.
*   **Mechanism:** It breaks a top-level KPI (e.g., Net Profit) into its contributing components (Revenue, COGS, OpEx).
*   **Path Selection:** Analyzes historical data to identify which sub-KPI has the highest probability of moving to achieve the goal.

### 3. AI Strategy Generation & Ranking
*   **Theoretical Goal:** Context-aware strategic brainstorming.
*   **Action Generation:** The AI generates specific, domain-relevant strategies (e.g., *"Negotiate with supplier X for a 5% discount on raw materials"*).
*   **Scoring & Ranking:** Each strategy is scored on effectiveness, cost efficiency, domain fit, and time to impact. Only the top-tier actions are presented.

### 4. Scenario Builder: Lean, Balanced, Premium
*   **Theoretical Goal:** Resource-conscious execution planning.
*   **Lean:** Bootstrapped approach requiring minimal investment.
*   **Balanced:** The standard industry-best-practice path.
*   **Premium:** Aggressive, resource-intensive path for rapid results.

### 5. Strategy Canvas & Location Localization
*   **Theoretical Goal:** Providing the final "Action Plan."
*   **Final Output:** A comprehensive Strategy Canvas that includes the goal, selected actions, scenarios, and a localized breakdown for specific branches/regions.

---
**Core Domain Impact:** Provides business leaders with a data-backed roadmap to achieve their goals, reducing the "Decision Fatigue" associated with complex data analytics.
