## 🗄️ Data Structure Expectations

To get the most out of the AI, it helps to understand what the AI looks for when classifying your data as Services.

### Core Entities
- **Primary Entity:** The central focus (e.g., Transactions, Users, Patients, Orders).
- **Secondary Entity:** The dimension to cut by (e.g., Demographics, Products, Locations).
- **Temporal Entity:** A date/time column allowing for time-series analysis.

### Quality Requirements
VistaraBI's Module 2 automatically cleans your data, but for the AI to perform complex correlations, ensure your foreign keys (e.g., matching a user ID in table A to table B) are consistent.

**Context:** An agency owner notices margins slipping.

**You:** *"Are any active projects currently over budget?"*

**AI:** *"Yes, the 'Website Redesign for CorpX' is currently 120% over its allocated hours budget."*

**You:** *"Which phase of the project burned the most hours?"*

**You:** *"Synthesize the financial impact of this overrun."*

**AI:** *"The overrun on CorpX has reduced the project's profit margin from an estimated 40% to -5% (a loss of $2,500). Overall agency margin for the month has dropped by 1.2% as a result."*
