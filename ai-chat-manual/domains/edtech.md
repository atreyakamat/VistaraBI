## 🗄️ Data Structure Expectations

To get the most out of the AI, it helps to understand what the AI looks for when classifying your data as EdTech.

### Core Entities
- **Primary Entity:** The central focus (e.g., Transactions, Users, Patients, Orders).
- **Secondary Entity:** The dimension to cut by (e.g., Demographics, Products, Locations).
- **Temporal Entity:** A date/time column allowing for time-series analysis.

### Quality Requirements
VistaraBI's Module 2 automatically cleans your data, but for the AI to perform complex correlations, ensure your foreign keys (e.g., matching a user ID in table A to table B) are consistent.
