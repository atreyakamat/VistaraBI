Conclusion and Future Scope

6.1 Conclusion
Business intelligence (BI) is a core component of modern business operations, rich with necessary operational insights. Automated dashboards, a key resource for small-to-medium scale enterprises, are vital for business growth due to their ability to reveal efficiency bottlenecks, forecasting horizons, and optimization opportunities. Ensuring clean ingestion standards is essential, as the dashboard’s output accuracy depends on the raw data quality. This research aims to change SMB business intelligence by implementing a modular, automated BI pipeline to process, clean, classify, compile, and reason over multi-domain transaction files based on schemas. By utilizing different analytic models, including keyword heuristic search, local LLM schema reasoning, and cloud-based API orchestrators, we classify, clean, and map transaction records into 8 business domains: E-Commerce, SaaS, EdTech, Retail, Services, Manufacturing, Healthcare, and Finance. Using a large test suite of 927 test cases allows for comprehensive validation of the models and engines, which are evaluated using metrics like F1-score, precision, and recall. The results show that among the configuration options, the Hybrid Governance Orchestrator outperforms them across all business domains, achieving a precision of 0.99, recall of 0.98, and an F1-score of 0.98 for E-Commerce datasets; a precision of 0.97, recall of 0.96, and an F1-score of 0.96 for SaaS datasets; and a precision of 0.96, recall of 0.95, and an F1-score of 0.95 for Retail datasets. The result of this research gives a possibility for automating dashboard creation in business environments by providing a rapid and accurate data mapping tool. Further, it can be integrated into live database feeds (e.g., Snowflake, BigQuery) for continuous automated reporting, thereby optimizing market competitiveness, efficiency, and data-driven productivity.

6.2 Future Scope
This innovative approach not only addresses the pressing need for automated data ingestion and dashboard creation but also offers a pathway towards self-service analytics. By reducing reliance on manual data engineering and optimizing query routing, it has the potential to enhance the overall efficiency and profitability of small and medium business operations. Furthermore, the integration of automated KPI schemas with external workflow systems and messaging channels (such as Slack, Microsoft Teams, and email automation via Resend) paves the way for a future where active monitoring and automated exception handling play a central role.
