# VistaraBI User Guide

Welcome to VistaraBI, the intelligent business analytics platform.

## Getting Started

1. **Upload Data:** Navigate to the "Sources" tab to upload your CSV, JSON, XML, or XLSX files. VistaraBI automatically infers data types and profiles the dataset.
2. **Data Quality:** Review the Data Purification and Quality dashboard to ensure your dataset receives an acceptable grade (A-F).
3. **Domain Classification:** VistaraBI will auto-detect your business domain (e.g., E-Commerce, Retail, SaaS). You can manually override this via the Settings tab.
4. **KPI Engine:** Once the domain is confirmed, the KPI Engine will automatically discover, validate, and execute relevant KPIs based on your data lineage.
5. **Dashboards:** View interactive dashboards generated from your KPIs. Use the AI command bar to ask questions or filter data using natural language.
6. **Goal Strategy (Module 7 & 8):** Define business goals and generate actionable strategies with Monte Carlo simulations.
7. **Reports:** Generate professional Executive Board PDF reports with AI-driven narrative.

## Advanced Features
- **AI Command Execution:** Ask "What caused the drop in sales?" to automatically run correlation and lag analyses.
- **Data Lineage:** Trace exactly how each KPI is computed, down to the original column and transformation rules.
- **GDPR Compliance:** Support for data export and the Right to Erasure.
