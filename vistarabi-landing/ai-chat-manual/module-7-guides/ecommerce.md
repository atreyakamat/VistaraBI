# E-Commerce Guide for Module 7: Goal Strategy Engine

## 1. Introduction to Prescriptive E-Commerce
In the fast-paced world of E-Commerce, descriptive analytics (knowing you sold 500 units last week) is insufficient. VistaraBI's Module 7 transforms E-Commerce platforms into proactive strategy generators. By understanding the intricate mathematical relationships between traffic, conversion rates, and basket sizes, Module 7 translates broad goals into tactical playbooks.

## 2. Core E-Commerce Mechanics in Module 7
The Goal Decomposer in Module 7 understands the fundamental equation of E-Commerce:
**Revenue = Traffic × Conversion Rate × Average Order Value (AOV)**

When an E-Commerce user sets a goal like "Increase revenue by 20%", Module 7 does not blindly suggest "spend more on ads." It mathematically decomposes the goal to show that a 20% revenue lift can be achieved by:
- Increasing Traffic by 10% AND
- Increasing AOV by 10%

This decomposition grounds AI-generated strategies in mathematical reality.

## 4. Deep Dive: A Strategy Canvas Example
Let's walk through what happens when an E-Commerce brand inputs: **"Increase AOV by 15% this quarter."**

### Stage 1 & 2: Parsing and Mapping
The engine maps "AOV" to the official blueprint KPI: `ec-aov`.

### Stage 3: Decomposing
To increase AOV, the engine highlights contributing factors:
- Increasing items per cart (Cross-selling).
- Increasing the average price per item (Premium product positioning).
- Decreasing reliance on deep discounts.

### Stages 4 & 5: Generation and Ranking
The AI generates strategies. The ranker scores them based on the E-Commerce domain, prioritizing high-speed, low-cost actions.
1.  **Dynamic Bundling (Confidence: 92%)**
2.  **Free Shipping Threshold Optimization (Confidence: 88%)**
3.  **Post-Purchase Upsell Flows (Confidence: 85%)**

### Stage 6: The Scenarios (Execution Plans)
For "Dynamic Bundling," Module 7 provides three execution paths:

**LEAN (Low Budget, DIY):**
- *Plan:* Manually group frequently bought together items into a single new SKU in Shopify/WooCommerce. Use a native discount code for the bundle.
- *Timeline:* 1 week.
- *Expected Lift:* 2-4% AOV.

**BALANCED (Standard Tools):**
- *Plan:* Install an algorithmic product recommendation app (like Rebuy or Wiser). Configure rules to suggest complementary items directly on the product detail page (PDP) and in the cart drawer.
- *Timeline:* 2-3 weeks.
- *Expected Lift:* 5-10% AOV.

**PREMIUM (High Investment):**
- *Plan:* Hire a CRO agency to implement a headless, AI-driven personalization engine (e.g., Dynamic Yield or Nosto) that curates real-time, user-specific bundles across the entire site journey, integrating with your CDP.
- *Timeline:* 2-3 months.
- *Expected Lift:* 12-18% AOV.

### Stage 7: Location & Segment Split
If the user operates in multiple regions:
- *California (High Performance):* Implement the Premium plan to maximize existing high traffic.
- *Texas (Low Performance):* Deploy the Lean manual bundling to test market appetite before investing in software.
