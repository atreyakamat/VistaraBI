# Retail Guide for Module 7: Goal Strategy Engine

## 1. Introduction to Prescriptive Retail
In brick-and-mortar and omnichannel Retail, foot traffic, inventory turnover, and square footage yield are the ultimate drivers of success. VistaraBI's Module 7 takes point-of-sale (POS) data, foot traffic analytics, and inventory counts to prescribe exact strategies for store managers and regional directors to optimize their floors.

## 2. Core Retail Mechanics in Module 7
The Goal Decomposer understands spatial and transactional equations:
**Sales per Square Foot = (Foot Traffic × Conversion Rate × AOV) / Store Square Footage**

If a regional director sets a goal like "Increase Sales per Sq Ft by 10%", Module 7 mathematically decomposes this into paths such as:
- Increasing Foot Traffic (Marketing/Window Displays) OR
- Increasing In-Store Conversion Rate (Staffing levels, store layout) OR
- Increasing AOV (Basket size via point-of-sale upselling).

## 3. Top Retail Use Cases & Prompts
Effective natural language goals for Retail in Module 7:

*   "Increase weekend foot traffic conversion rate by 5%."
*   "Boost inventory turnover ratio to 4.0 this year."
*   "Increase Sales per Square Foot by 15% in Q4."
*   "Reduce stockout rate on top-selling SKUs by 10%."
*   "Increase average items per basket to 3.5."

## 4. Deep Dive: A Strategy Canvas Example
Let's analyze the input: **"Increase Sales per Square Foot by 15% in Q4."**

### Stage 1 & 2: Parsing and Mapping
The engine maps "Sales per Square Foot" to the blueprint KPI: `rt-sales-sqft`.

### Stage 3: Decomposing
To increase this metric without expanding the store, the engine highlights:
- Optimizing high-margin product placement (endcaps).
- Increasing staff-to-customer ratios during peak hours.
- Implementing localized in-store promotions.

### Stages 4 & 5: Generation and Ranking
The AI, tuned for retail operations, generates and ranks strategies:
1.  **Dynamic Endcap Merchandising based on POS Data (Confidence: 92%)**
2.  **Predictive Staff Scheduling for Peak Hours (Confidence: 87%)**
3.  **BOPIS (Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store) Upsell Training (Confidence: 81%)**

### Stage 6: The Scenarios (Execution Plans)
For "Dynamic Endcap Merchandising," Module 7 builds three tiers:

**LEAN (Low Budget, DIY):**
- *Plan:* Print a weekly report of the top 5 high-margin SKUs from Module 5. Instruct store managers to manually move these items to the front-of-store endcaps every Monday morning.
- *Timeline:* 1 week.
- *Expected Impact:* 2-4% sales lift.

**BALANCED (Standard Tools):**
- *Plan:* Use a retail planogram software integrated with VistaraBI to generate auto-updating store layouts. Send digital alerts to staff tablets when an endcap SKU drops below a certain velocity threshold.
- *Timeline:* 3-4 weeks.
- *Expected Impact:* 5-8% sales lift.

**PREMIUM (High Investment):**
- *Plan:* Install digital smart-shelves and electronic ink price tags. Use AI to dynamically adjust pricing and digital signage based on real-time foot traffic and weather data to push high-margin items dynamically.
- *Timeline:* 3-6 months.
- *Expected Impact:* 10-15%+ sales lift.

### Stage 7: Location & Segment Split
This is highly relevant for Retail:
- *Flagship Stores (High Traffic):* Deploy Premium digital smart-shelves.
- *Suburban Stores (Low Traffic):* Deploy Lean manual endcap rotation.

## 5. Domain-Specific AI Considerations
The AI evaluates strategies with an understanding of physical retail constraints:
- **Shrinkage/Theft:** Factoring in loss prevention when suggesting open-display strategies.
- **Supply Chain:** Understanding that you cannot promote what is not in the backroom.
- **Seasonality:** Holiday spikes, back-to-school rushes.
