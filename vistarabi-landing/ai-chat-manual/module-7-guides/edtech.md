# EdTech Guide for Module 7: Goal Strategy Engine

## 1. Introduction to Prescriptive EdTech
In Educational Technology (EdTech), outcomes are measured in engagement, retention, and course completion, not just immediate revenue. VistaraBI's Module 7 allows educational institutions, course creators, and LMS platforms to turn static learner data into prescriptive strategies designed to maximize both learning outcomes and subscription longevity.

## 2. Core EdTech Mechanics in Module 7
The Goal Decomposer understands the EdTech growth loop:
**Active Learners = Total Signups × Course Completion Rate × Subscription Retention Rate**

If an EdTech provider sets a goal like "Increase Active Learners by 15%", Module 7 mathematically decomposes this to show that success relies on:
- Boosting Course Completion Rate (reducing drop-offs) AND
- Increasing Subscription Retention (keeping users paying/active after course 1).

## 3. Top EdTech Use Cases & Prompts

## 4. Deep Dive: A Strategy Canvas Example
Let's analyze what happens when an EdTech platform inputs: **"Reduce student churn by 15% this quarter."**

### Stage 1 & 2: Parsing and Mapping
The engine maps "student churn" to the blueprint KPI: `edtech-churn-rate`.

### Stage 3: Decomposing
To reduce churn, the engine identifies contributing factors:
- Increasing weekly engagement minutes.
- Improving assessment pass rates (reducing frustration).
- Increasing community interaction/forum posts.

### Stages 4 & 5: Generation and Ranking
The AI, configured for EdTech, brainstorms and ranks strategies:
1.  **Automated Milestone Nudges (Confidence: 94%)**
2.  **Gamified Study Streaks (Confidence: 89%)**
3.  **Peer Mentorship Matching (Confidence: 82%)**

### Stage 6: The Scenarios (Execution Plans)
For "Automated Milestone Nudges," Module 7 builds three tiers:

**LEAN (Low Budget, DIY):**
- *Plan:* Export a list of users inactive for 7 days. Send a manual, personalized email campaign encouraging them to complete the next module.
- *Timeline:* 3-5 days.
- *Expected Lift:* 2-5% retention improvement.

**BALANCED (Standard Tools):**
- *Plan:* Set up automated triggers in your CRM (like HubSpot or ActiveCampaign) tied to LMS webhooks. Send automated SMS/email nudges when a user misses a scheduled lesson.
- *Timeline:* 2 weeks.
- *Expected Lift:* 7-12% retention improvement.

**PREMIUM (High Investment):**
- *Plan:* Integrate an AI-driven learning companion (e.g., a custom chatbot) directly into the LMS that proactively messages students with personalized study tips when their engagement metrics drop.
- *Timeline:* 2-4 months.
- *Expected Lift:* 15-20% retention improvement.

### Stage 7: Location & Segment Split
If demographic data is available:
- *University Segment (High Engagement):* Deploy the Premium AI companion to scale personalized help.
- *Bootcamp Segment (Low Engagement):* Use Lean manual interventions to directly uncover why users are dropping off.

## 5. Domain-Specific AI Considerations
The AI evaluates strategies with an understanding of EdTech nuances:
- **Pedagogy vs. Gamification:** Balancing serious learning with engagement tactics.
- **Seasonality:** Understanding academic calendars (Midterms, Summer breaks).
- **Compliance:** Avoiding strategies that violate student data privacy regulations (FERPA, GDPR).

2. **Set a Goal:** Open Module 7 and ask: "Increase completion rate of Course X by 10%."
3. **Execute:** Implement the suggested 'Balanced' strategy to add automated check-ins for that specific course.
