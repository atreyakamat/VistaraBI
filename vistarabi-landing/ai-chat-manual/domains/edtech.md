# EdTech AI Chat Guide

Welcome to the EdTech (Educational Technology) playbook for the VistaraBI AI Chat Assistant! If your organization manages students, online courses, grades, and platform engagement, this guide will help you extract the best insights.

## 🎓 How the AI Understands EdTech

The AI looks at your data through the lens of student success and institutional efficiency:
- **Completion Rate:** The percentage of enrolled students who actually finish the course.
- **Engagement Time:** Hours or minutes spent watching video lectures, reading materials, or participating in forums.
- **Assessment Performance:** Average grades, quiz scores, and pass/fail rates.
- **Drop-out / Attrition Rate:** Where and when students abandon the curriculum.

## 🗣️ Scenario 1: Course Performance

Quickly understand how different curricula are performing.

**You:** *"What is the average completion rate across all our courses?"*
**AI:** *"The average course completion rate is currently 68%."*

**You:** *"Which specific course has the highest drop-out rate?"*
**AI:** *"The 'Advanced Data Science' course has the highest drop-out rate at 42%, with most students abandoning the course around Module 4."*

**You:** *"Show me a bar chart comparing the average final grades of all our courses."*
**AI:** *[Generates a bar chart sorting courses by average student grade]*

## 🕵️ Scenario 2: Student Success Correlations

Use the AI to figure out *why* students succeed or fail.

**You:** *"Why are students failing the Intro to Python exam?"*
**AI:** *"I see an anomaly where the failure rate for the 'Intro to Python' final exam spiked to 35% this semester, compared to the historical average of 15%."*

**You:** *"Does time spent on the platform correlate with higher assessment scores for that class?"*
**AI:** *"Yes. There is a very strong positive correlation (0.88) between total minutes spent in the 'Interactive Coding Environment' and the final exam score. Students who bypassed the coding exercises were overwhelmingly the ones who failed."*

## 🎨 Scenario 3: Instructor and Cohort Dashboards

**You:** *"Create a scatter plot showing student age vs. course completion time."*
**AI:** *[Generates a scatter plot helping you identify demographic learning speeds]*
**You:** *"Filter my entire dashboard to only show data for the 'Fall 2025 Bootcamp' cohort."*
**AI:** *[Instantly updates all charts and KPIs to reflect only that specific cohort]*
**You:** *"Add a KPI card showing the 'Highest Rated Instructor'."*

## 🚀 Advanced Commands to Try

- **Synthesis:** *"Synthesize the engagement metrics for the last 30 days. Are students becoming more or less active?"*
- **Onboarding Drop-off:** *"What percentage of new signups complete their profile setup within 24 hours?"*
- **Content Review:** *"Which individual video lesson has the highest rate of pausing and re-watching?"*
