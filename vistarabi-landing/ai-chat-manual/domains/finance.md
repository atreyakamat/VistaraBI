# Finance AI Chat Guide

Welcome to the Finance playbook for the VistaraBI AI Chat Assistant! If your organization deals with banking, lending, portfolios, or fintech transactions, this guide will help you manage risk and track asset velocity.

## 💰 How the AI Understands Finance

The AI focuses on money movement, risk assessment, and portfolio health:
- **AUM (Assets Under Management):** The total market value of the investments managed.
- **NPL (Non-Performing Loans):** The rate of loans that are in default or close to being in default.
- **Transaction Velocity:** The volume and speed of money moving through the system.
- **Risk Scoring:** Evaluating the danger associated with specific accounts or transactions.

## 🗣️ Scenario 1: Portfolio & Transaction Overview

Get an instant read on the market and your ledgers.

**You:** *"What is the total value of our asset portfolio right now?"*
**AI:** *"The total value of your active asset portfolio is currently $45.2 Million."*

**You:** *"Show me the total transaction volume for the last 24 hours."*
**AI:** *"In the last 24 hours, there were 12,450 transactions totaling $3.1 Million in volume."*

**You:** *"Graph the daily net deposits (total deposits minus total withdrawals) for Q1 in a line chart."*
**AI:** *[Generates a line chart showing liquidity trends over the quarter]*

## 🕵️ Scenario 2: Fraud & Risk Detection

Use the AI's anomaly detection engine to spot issues before they escalate.

**You:** *"Are there any highly anomalous transactions from yesterday?"*
**AI:** *"Yes, I detected 4 anomalies. There were 4 international wire transfers exceeding $50,000 from accounts that typically average less than $500 in daily movement."*

**You:** *"What is our current rate of Non-Performing Loans (NPL)?"*
**AI:** *"Your current NPL rate is 3.8%."*

**You:** *"Is there a correlation between the applicant's initial risk score and loan default?"*
**AI:** *"Yes, there is a very strong negative correlation (-0.89). Applicants who scored below 620 on your internal risk metric accounted for 85% of all defaults this year."*
