# Retail AI Chat Guide

Welcome to the Retail playbook for the VistaraBI AI Chat Assistant! If your business deals with physical inventory, brick-and-mortar stores, point-of-sale systems, and supply chains, this guide is for you.

## 🏪 How the AI Understands Retail

The AI focuses on moving boxes, managing shelves, and optimizing floor space:
- **Inventory Turnover:** How many times you sell and replace your entire stock over a period.
- **Sell-Through Rate:** The ratio of the amount of inventory sold to the amount of inventory received.
- **Foot Traffic:** The physical volume of people entering your stores.
- **Gross Margin Return on Investment (GMROI):** Analyzing whether your inventory is actually making you money.

## 🗣️ Scenario 1: Multi-Store Check-In

Manage multiple locations without opening a dozen spreadsheets.

**You:** *"What were our total retail sales across all locations yesterday?"*
**AI:** *"Total sales across your 12 locations yesterday were $84,200."*

**You:** *"Which store location had the highest revenue?"*
**AI:** *"The 'Downtown Metro' store generated the highest revenue at $15,400."*

**You:** *"Compare the Downtown store's sales to the Suburban store's sales in a line chart for the last 7 days."*
**AI:** *[Generates a multi-line chart tracking daily sales for both specific locations]*

## 🕵️ Scenario 2: Supply Chain & Inventory Issues

Don't wait until shelves are empty to realize there's a problem.

**You:** *"Are any of our top-selling items hitting their reorder point?"*
**AI:** *"Yes. 4 of your top 20 items have fallen below their 15-day reorder threshold, including 'Premium Winter Coats' and 'Thermal Gloves'."*

**You:** *"Why are we so low on Winter Coats?"*
**AI:** *"There is an anomaly in your sales data. Sales for Winter Coats spiked 300% last week. Furthermore, looking at your Supplier data, 'Supplier A' currently has an average lead time delay of +8 days."*

**You:** *"Is there a correlation between local weather data (if uploaded) and this foot traffic spike?"*
**AI:** *"Yes, I found a strong negative correlation (-0.85) between the local temperature drop and the spike in foot traffic and winter apparel sales."*

## 🎨 Scenario 3: Merchandising Dashboards

**You:** *"Create a bar chart showing inventory turnover rates by product category."*
**AI:** *[Generates a chart highlighting fast-moving vs. stagnant categories]*
**You:** *"Add a KPI card to the dashboard showing our 'Dead Stock Value' (items not sold in 90+ days)."*
**AI:** *[Calculates the tied-up capital and displays it prominently]*
**You:** *"Filter the dashboard to only show data for the 'Spring Collection' promotion."*

## 🚀 Advanced Commands to Try

- **Synthesis:** *"Synthesize our weekend performance. Did the 'Buy One Get One' promo actually increase our total gross margin?"*
- **Cashier Performance:** *"What is our average transaction value per employee/cashier?"*
- **Shrinkage:** *"Are there anomalies in our stock counts that might indicate inventory shrinkage or theft?"*
