# E-Commerce AI Chat Guide

Welcome to the E-Commerce playbook for the VistaraBI AI Chat Assistant! If your business involves selling goods online, shipping products, and managing shopping carts, this guide will show you how to leverage the AI to its fullest potential.

## 🛒 How the AI Understands E-Commerce

When you upload e-commerce data (like Shopify, WooCommerce, or Magento exports), the AI automatically recognizes standard industry concepts:
- **AOV (Average Order Value):** The average amount spent per transaction.
- **Cart Abandonment Rate:** The percentage of shoppers who add items to their cart but leave without purchasing.
- **CLV (Customer Lifetime Value):** The total amount of money a customer is expected to spend with your business over their lifetime.
- **SKU Performance:** Tracking product-level sales, velocity, and stock levels.

## 🗣️ Scenario 1: The Daily Check-In

You don't need to dig through reports every morning. Just ask the AI.

**You:** *"How did we perform yesterday?"*
**AI:** *"Yesterday, you processed 420 orders for a total revenue of $18,500. This is a 5% increase compared to the same day last week."*

**You:** *"What was the Average Order Value for those orders?"*
**AI:** *"The AOV for yesterday was $44.04."*

**You:** *"How does that compare to our monthly average?"*
**AI:** *"Your AOV for the entire month is currently $39.50, so yesterday's AOV was significantly higher (+11.5%)."*

## 🕵️ Scenario 2: Root Cause Analysis (The "Why")

When something looks wrong, the AI can cross-reference multiple tables (e.g., Orders, Shipping, Reviews) to find the cause.

**You:** *"Why did our sales drop last Thursday?"*
**AI:** *"I detected an anomaly on Thursday. Total sales dropped by 22%. By analyzing your web logs and cart data, I found that the Cart Abandonment Rate spiked to 85% (normally 60%)."*

**You:** *"Is there a correlation between that spike and shipping delays?"*
**AI:** *"Yes. Running a correlation analysis reveals a strong positive correlation (0.82) between shipping delays reported in the 'Fulfillment' table and abandoned carts. It appears a carrier delay notice was active on your checkout page that day."*

## 🎨 Scenario 3: Dashboard Customization

Build your perfect e-commerce command center using just your words.

**You:** *"Create a bar chart showing our top 10 selling products this month."*
**AI:** *[Generates a bar chart of top SKUs by revenue]*
**You:** *"Actually, change that to show profit margin instead of total revenue."*
**AI:** *[Updates the chart to reflect gross profit margin per SKU]*
**You:** *"Perfect. Pin this to my main dashboard and filter the whole dashboard to only show 'Electronics'."*

## 🚀 Advanced Commands to Try

- **Synthesis:** *"Synthesize our Black Friday weekend performance into 3 key takeaways."*
- **Cohort Analysis:** *"Show me the retention rate of customers who made their first purchase in January."*
- **Predictive/Trend:** *"Based on the last 30 days, are we on track to hit our $500k Q4 revenue goal?"*
