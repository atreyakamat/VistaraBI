# SaaS AI Chat Guide

Welcome to the SaaS (Software as a Service) playbook for the VistaraBI AI Chat Assistant! If your business relies on recurring subscriptions, user retention, and software metrics, this guide will show you how to chat with your data.

## 💻 How the AI Understands SaaS

The AI natively understands the subscription economy. It looks for recurring patterns, account statuses, and cohort behaviors:
- **MRR / ARR:** Monthly or Annual Recurring Revenue.
- **Churn Rate:** The rate at which customers cancel their subscriptions.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** How much it costs to acquire a new paying user.
- **DAU / MAU:** Daily or Monthly Active Users (product engagement).
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** The expected revenue a single user generates over their subscription lifespan.

## 🗣️ Scenario 1: Tracking the Pulse

Keep your finger on the most critical SaaS metrics using natural language.

**You:** *"What is our current MRR?"*
**AI:** *"Your current MRR sits at $245,000 as of today."*

**You:** *"How much of that is new MRR added this month vs expansion MRR?"*
**AI:** *"This month, you added $12,000 in New MRR (new signups) and $5,500 in Expansion MRR (existing users upgrading their plans)."*

**You:** *"Chart our MRR growth over the last 12 months as a line graph."*
**AI:** *[Generates a smooth line chart showing MRR trajectory]*

## 🕵️ Scenario 2: Investigating Churn

Churn is the enemy of SaaS. Let the AI help you fight it.

**You:** *"Did our churn rate increase last month?"*
**AI:** *"Yes, your churn rate increased from 3.2% in August to 4.5% in September."*

**You:** *"Is there a correlation between users who churned and their platform usage?"*
**AI:** *"Yes. I ran a correlation analysis between the 'Usage Logs' table and the 'Cancellations' table. There is a strong negative correlation (-0.78) between DAU (Daily Active Usage) in the 14 days prior to cancellation. Essentially, users who stop logging in are highly likely to churn two weeks later."*

**You:** *"Show me a list of current active users who haven't logged in for 10 days."*
**AI:** *[Generates a data table of "At-Risk" users for your success team to contact]*
