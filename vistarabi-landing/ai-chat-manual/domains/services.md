# Services AI Chat Guide

Welcome to the Services playbook for the VistaraBI AI Chat Assistant! If your business sells time, expertise, or custom projects (like agencies, consultancies, or law firms), this guide will help you manage your resources and margins effectively.

## 🧾 How the AI Understands Services

The AI focuses on the delicate balance of time, money, and human resources:
- **Utilization Rate:** The percentage of available hours spent on billable client work versus internal tasks.
- **Project Margin:** The actual profit of a project compared to its budget.
- **Realization Rate:** How much of the logged billable work actually got invoiced to the client.
- **Accounts Receivable:** Managing overdue invoices and cash flow.

## 🗣️ Scenario 1: Resource & Time Tracking

Stay on top of who is doing what, and how much it's worth.

**You:** *"What is our team's overall utilization rate this week?"*
**AI:** *"The team's utilization rate is currently 72%, which is slightly below your target of 80%."*

**You:** *"Which employee has logged the most non-billable hours this month?"*
**AI:** *"John Smith has logged the most non-billable hours (35 hours), primarily categorized under 'Internal Training' and 'Sales Support'."*

**You:** *"Create a pie chart showing a breakdown of our revenue by service type (e.g., Design vs. Development)."*
**AI:** *[Generates a pie chart based on your invoice line items]*

## 🕵️ Scenario 2: Project Profitability

Don't let scope creep destroy your margins.

**You:** *"Show me a list of all projects that are currently over budget."*
**AI:** *"There are 3 projects currently over budget: 'Alpha Website Redesign' (115%), 'Beta SEO Campaign' (105%), and 'Gamma App Audit' (102%)."*

**You:** *"Why is the Alpha project so far over budget?"*
**AI:** *"Analyzing the timesheets for the 'Alpha' project reveals an anomaly. There was a 400% spike in 'Revisions/QA' hours logged by the design team over the last two weeks."*

## 🎨 Scenario 3: Cash Flow Dashboard

**You:** *"Add a KPI card showing our total Accounts Receivable."*
**AI:** *[Adds a card showing total unpaid invoices]*
**You:** *"Now add a bar chart showing those unpaid invoices broken down by aging (30, 60, 90+ days)."*
**AI:** *[Generates an aging report chart]*

## 🚀 Advanced Commands to Try

- **Synthesis:** *"Synthesize our financial health based on current invoices and utilization. Are we risking a cash flow crunch?"*
- **Client Profitability:** *"Rank our clients by average profit margin."*
- **Forecasting:** *"Based on currently contracted hours, what is our projected revenue for next month?"*
