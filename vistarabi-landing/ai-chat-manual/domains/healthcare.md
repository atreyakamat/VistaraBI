# Healthcare AI Chat Guide

Welcome to the Healthcare playbook for the VistaraBI AI Chat Assistant! If you manage a clinic, hospital, dental office, or care facility, this guide will help you navigate patient flows, appointments, and billing data securely.

## 🏥 How the AI Understands Healthcare

The AI focuses on patient experience, facility utilization, and administrative efficiency:
- **Wait Times:** The duration between patient check-in and seeing a provider.
- **No-Show Rate:** The percentage of booked appointments where the patient did not arrive.
- **Bed / Room Occupancy:** How fully utilized the physical facility is at any given time.
- **Claim Denial Rate:** The percentage of invoices rejected by insurance providers.

## 🗣️ Scenario 1: Clinic Flow & Appointments

Manage the daily chaos of scheduling and patient throughput.

**You:** *"How many total appointments were completed last week?"*
**AI:** *"Last week, your facility completed 412 patient appointments."*

**You:** *"What is our current appointment no-show rate?"*
**AI:** *"The average no-show rate for this month is currently 14%."*

**You:** *"Which doctor or department handles the highest number of daily appointments?"*
**AI:** *"Dr. Smith handles the highest volume, averaging 28 patient visits per day, primarily in the General Practice department."*

## 🕵️ Scenario 2: Investigating Wait Times

Long wait times lead to poor patient satisfaction. Let the AI find the bottlenecks.

**You:** *"What was our average patient wait time in the ER today?"*
**AI:** *"The average wait time in the ER today was 45 minutes, which is an anomaly compared to your typical 25-minute average."*

**You:** *"Is there a correlation between the time of day and the length of patient wait times?"*
**AI:** *"Yes. I found a strong positive correlation (0.78) between appointments scheduled between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM and extended wait times. It appears the clinic consistently falls behind schedule immediately following the lunch hour."*

## 🎨 Scenario 3: Billing & Administrative Dashboard

**You:** *"Create a pie chart showing the breakdown of billing amounts by insurance provider."*
**AI:** *[Generates a pie chart of payer distribution]*
**You:** *"Add a KPI card showing our current Insurance Claim Denial Rate."*

**You:** *"Filter the dashboard to only show data for the 'Pediatrics' wing."*

## 🚀 Advanced Commands to Try

- **Synthesis:** *"Synthesize our appointment data for the week. Are there any concerning trends in cancellations?"*
- **Follow-ups:** *"How many follow-up appointments were scheduled as a direct result of yesterday's initial visits?"*
- **Treatment Anomalies:** *"Are there any anomalies in the prescription rates for antibiotics this month compared to last year?"*
