6.1 Conclusion

Fruits are basic components of a balanced diet which are rich with necessary nutrients.
Cashew apple, a key non-seasonal fruit in India, is vital for coastal farmers due to its
diverse applications and export potential. Ensuring high-quality standards is essential, as
the fruit’s market value depends on its overall quality. This research aims to change the
cashew industry by implementing the deep learning technique to grade cashew apple
fruit’s quality based on the images. By utilizing different deep learning models, including
AlexNet, ResNet, MobileNet and VGG19, and implementing different image processing
methods in order to classify cashew apple images into three classes: ripe, unripe, and
over-ripe. Using a large dataset gathered from two farms allows for training and
validation of the models which are evaluated using metrics like F1 score, recall, and
precision which evaluate the effectiveness of the deep learning models. The results shows
that among the other models under consideration VGG19 outperforms them across all the
three grades of Cashew Apple, achieving a precision of 0.81, recall of 0.95 and an F1-
score of 0.87 for Overripe grade; a precision of 0.79, recall of 0.79 and an F1-score of
0.79 for ripe grade; and and a precision of 0.95, recall of 0.79 and an F1-score of 0.86 for
unripe grade. The result of this research gives a possibility for automating fruit quality
assessment in cashew industry by providing a rapid and accurate quality assessment tool.
Further it can be integrated into drones for automated harvesting, thereby optimizing
market competitiveness, product quality control, and efficiency in cashew fruit grading
leading to precision and productivity improvement.
